# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — approval alpha

Read-only inventory index, deterministic previews, BLU/BLM policies, indicators,
data provenance, validation, Horizon review, stable profile-scoped manual pins,
and the fail-closed action-mechanics contract.

## Phase 1 — reviewed execution bridge

Add an opt-in executor behind an explicit safety gate after approval. Consume the
resolved cache, preserve TP, coalesce swaps, expose every decision, and retain a
global kill switch. Integrate with LuAshitacast through a narrow bridge first.
The executor must consume resolved pin decisions; it may not reinterpret or
bypass their legality and weapon-lock status.

## Phase 2 — complete level-75 data

Expand the Horizon catalog, hidden effects, level-sync legality, augment variants,
set bonuses, target families, and patch provenance. Add exact solving where the
candidate space permits and validated dominance pruning elsewhere.
Expand the action-mechanics registry only from reviewed per-outcome evidence.

## Phase 3 — all live jobs

Add action metadata and policies for every live job. Keep job-specific systems,
such as COR elemental shots and NIN elemental wheel behavior, explicit rather than
forcing them into generic stat weights.

## Phase 4 — progression and upgrades

Generate best-owned sets and unowned upgrade ladders at each effective level,
with source-backed reasons and acquisition priority.
